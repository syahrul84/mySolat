# Mac App Store: mySolat

Same App Store entry as the iPhone app (app id 6831867566, com.ginlabs.mysolat).
Paste these into the macOS version in App Store Connect.

## English

**Subtitle** (shared with the iPhone app)
> JAKIM prayer times & reminders

**Promotional text**
> Official JAKIM prayer times in your Mac's menu bar, with a live countdown, reminders you won't miss, and desktop widgets. Works offline.

**Keywords**
> waktu solat,jakim,prayer times,solat,muslim,islam,menu bar,imsak,malaysia,widget,zon,subuh,maghrib

**Description**
> mySolat keeps the official JAKIM prayer times in your Mac's menu bar, reminds you before each solat, and stays out of your way. Simple and private: no account, no ads, no tracking.
>
> MENU BAR
> • The next solat and when it enters, always in view
> • Show the prayer name and time, a countdown, or just the icon
> • Click for the whole day, with the Hijri date and a live countdown
>
> REMINDERS
> • A heads-up before each solat, from 1 to 120 minutes, so you have time for wudhu
> • A popup that stays on screen until you dismiss it, without stealing focus from your typing
> • Regular Mac notifications alongside the popup
> • Choose which solat to be reminded for
> • Quiet while your Mac is locked or asleep: if you just missed one, it shows when you come back
>
> WIDGETS
> • Small, medium and large widgets for your desktop and Notification Center
>
> PRAYER TIMES
> • Official JAKIM (e-solat) times for all 60 zones in Malaysia
> • Pick your district, or let mySolat find it from your location
> • At least 30 days saved, so it works offline
>
> Runs natively on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
>
> mySolat is an independent app, not affiliated with JAKIM or any government agency. Malaysian prayer times come from JAKIM's e-solat (www.e-solat.gov.my) through the free api.waktusolat.app service.

## Notes for the reviewer

> mySolat is a menu bar app: it has no Dock icon or main window. After launch, click the mySolat item in the menu bar at the top of the screen to see today's prayer times. Settings are in that menu. In Settings > Location, choose any zone, such as SGR01 (Kuala Lumpur area).
>
> Location: used only when the user taps Detect in the zone picker, once, to find their prayer zone. It is not stored.
>
> Reminder popups: a small panel appears before and at each prayer time and stays until dismissed.
>
> Widgets: add Prayer Times from the macOS widget gallery. No sign-in is required.
